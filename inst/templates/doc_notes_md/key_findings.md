The *Key findings* summary is generated from a fixed template, so that its structure and ordering are
identical for all samples. Only findings that were assessed are listed, and each list shows at most
five items (remaining items are indicated as *+n more*). Variants are labeled by gene symbol and protein
change (or coding DNA change for non-coding variants), with zygosity indicated for homozygous variants.
Within a list, variants are ordered alphabetically by gene symbol, then by alteration.

**Leading findings**

- __Pathogenic / likely pathogenic variants__ in the cancer predisposition genes of the virtual panel,
   pathogenic before likely pathogenic, with the number of variants asserted by ClinVar and by CPSR
   (ACMG/AMP criteria). If none are found, this is stated explicitly.

**Supporting findings** (in this order)

- __Variant classification__: number of variants, and of affected genes among the evaluated cancer
  predisposition genes, by classification (pathogenic/likely pathogenic, variants of uncertain
  significance (VUS), benign/likely benign)
- __Secondary findings__ (if assessed): pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants in the ACMG secondary
  findings genes
- __Genomic biomarkers__: variants with biomarker evidence of level A or B (validated or clinical
  evidence), with the types of evidence (predictive, prognostic, diagnostic, predisposing); if only
  lower-level evidence (C-E) is found, the number of variants is given
- __Pharmacogenomics__ (if assessed): CPIC phenotypes (with diplotype) for genes with alleles of altered
  function, and the drugs with CPIC prescribing recommendations, ordered by gene symbol
